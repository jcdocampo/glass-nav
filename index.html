PALAWAN

Where the blue turns green.

Limestone cliffs. Clear water. Quiet shores.

Palawan brings together steep limestone landscapes and water that shifts from pale turquoise to deep emerald. Along the shore, sunlight catches the ripples and casts the palms' shadows across the sand.

It is one of the many ways the Philippines surprises you with color, even before you leave the water's edge.

THE CORDILLERA

Green, from one hillside to the next.

In the northern mountains, rice terraces trace the shape of the land. Water catches the sky between rows of young rice, while the hills fade into morning mist.

The landscape changes with the light, revealing a quieter palette of fresh greens, warm earth, and distant blue ridges.

ALBAY

A little more color.
